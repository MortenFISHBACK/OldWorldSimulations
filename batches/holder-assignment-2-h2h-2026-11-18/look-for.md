# Army-wide objective holders, second try (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn, and at each
deployment drop, that gives every objective (markers, the hill, its own baggage train) to the unit that gives up least to hold it.
This second try uses the game's own control rule in that table: the nearest able unit holds, a unit holds only one objective, and a
nearer enemy takes it. Ties go to the cheaper unit, a unit falling back no longer steps off its objective without counting it, and the
"stays empty" line now says why. The other side picks its holders one unit at a time, with the old fixed lines.

- Does the unit the table names actually reach its objective and score it that turn?
- Are more marker turns scored on the new side, and by cheaper units?
- Do fewer units stand idle next to their own baggage train?
- Does a dear unit still walk onto a marker while a cheap Core unit stands idle?
