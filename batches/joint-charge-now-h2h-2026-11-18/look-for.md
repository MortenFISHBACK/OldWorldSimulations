# Joint charges now, on against off (2026-11-18, batch A)

Both sides play the new pricing with chances worked out from the board. The only difference: on one side, a unit that charges an enemy a
friend also charges (or could charge) is priced by the fight of both units together, and a follow-up or pursuit is priced with the friends
in that fight. On the other side a second charger is priced by its own figure only.

What to watch: does the "on" side send a second unit into a fight a friend charges, more often than the other side? When it claims a joint
charge, do both units really reach the fight, and is the first round won as often as it claimed? Does it lose more units in flank or rear
by charging together?
