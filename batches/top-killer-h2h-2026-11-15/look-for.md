# Top-killer head-to-head (2026-11-15)

The same 144 games twice, the same pairs as the staying-to-shoot head-to-head. In every game ONE side plays with the shooting and missile
changes and the other side plays as today's main. In the first half the changes play Mine, in the second half Opponent.

The changes: shooters and missile wizards value the volleys of the next two turns when they choose to stay or move; they take their best
move without marching when they can still shoot or cast; a shot at a big enemy counts what that enemy would do to the army; shooters and
wizards share one kill over this turn and the next. New since the last batch (the top-killer fix): a big lord's share of the wounds shrinks
when the shot cannot finish it, and only one charge of its damage is counted over the turns.

Watch for: which side wins more often and by how much; war machines and missiles going at the enemy's most dangerous unit (it was 31%
of free shots before the fix); the changed side's gunlines standing to shoot instead of marching; the "Rules off" line in each game saying
which side played without the changes.
