# Try 13: one assumed move per enemy unit, promises by the unit's own sum, a second look, against simpler pricing (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board and the army-wide move table,
made again after the charges, with shooting chosen after the moves. New in this try: the table assumes ONE action for each enemy unit
(his one move), read by every row and checked next turn; a promised charge counts only when the unit's own sum next turn would take it;
and with every row in place a charge now that is worth more than the unit's row is made (second look). The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: do promised charges now come (try 12: 85 claimed in 100, about 25 came)? How often did the enemy do what was assumed?
How did the second-look charges go? Do two units charge one target together and both get in? Are held markers kept as claimed?
