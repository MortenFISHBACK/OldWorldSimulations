# Walk 2 head-to-head (2026-11-22)

Walk 2: when the computer works out how many turns a unit needs to reach an objective, it walks the real ground only for
objectives it could reach within 2 turns; farther ones are counted by the straight line. This makes the first plan about 17%
shorter. One side has Walk 2, the other walks the real ground to every objective; the same 72 pairs, both ways.

Watch: whether the side with Walk 2 wins less (VP margin); "NEAR WALKS ONLY" notes on that side's objective rows; far units that
march toward an objective the long way round; minutes a game and the length of the first plan.
