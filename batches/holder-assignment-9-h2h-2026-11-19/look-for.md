# Army-wide objective holders, batch 9: one route for a walk (2026-11-19)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it, with every objective counted by the odds. New in this try: a walk to a marker
counts its turns with the very moves the unit is offered, stop after stop (one route), a marching unit only wheels (no sideways
slide at full march), a walk is called late only from where the unit now stands, and a walker moves along its route instead of
standing. The other side has all of this off and picks holders one unit at a time.

- Do units walking to a marker now arrive when they said, keep the same marker the turn after, and score?
- How far do far walkers move each turn compared with what the walk needs?
- When a walker has no row the turn after, what reason does the log give?
- Do table quarter, special feature and Herdstone claims still come true?
