# Try 10: the army-wide move table and joint charges against simpler pricing (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board, and new in this try: the
whole army's moves are priced together in one table (a row per unit, each priced against the others), the cheaper unit gives way on
contested ground, objective holders are picked army-wide, and two units can charge one target together, priced at the joint figure
(the enemy strikes back once, not once against every charger). The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: do two units charge one target together, and does the joint fight go as claimed? Does a unit that promised a charge
make the march and then charge? Do chargers get in each other's way? Do the points won per charged fight match what was expected?
