# Job framework on v off (2026-11-13)

The same 144 games twice on one build: once with the new job framework (best spot for each unit: shooting, next turn's charge, holding,
support, minus risk; screens, redirects and lures only when they work this turn; the defensive army; the lost charge; random movers by
their figures; stable roles; best facing), once with all of it switched off.

Watch for: units moving to a spot from which they charge next turn instead of walking straight at the enemy; shooters moving and
shooting before marching, and falling back with a Reserve Move; defensive armies (infantry and war machines that out-shoot the enemy)
holding back and only fighting when they are very likely to win; chaff screening only when it really blocks shooting this turn;
random movers (Doomwheels, Fanatics and the like) turned off the straight line; the new job colours on the board.
