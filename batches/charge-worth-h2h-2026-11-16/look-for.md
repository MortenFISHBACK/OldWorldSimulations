# Charge worth head-to-head (2026-11-16)

One side plays with three changes; the other side plays as main.
1. Honest charge odds: the six fixes to how the computer figures its chance of winning a charge.
2. The charge band: a charge that wins 55-75% alone asks a friend for help when together they win 76% or more, never into overkill.
3. Honest worth (new): a charge now counts what it costs the charger by its odds. A charger that wins its first round loses about 12% of
   its points over the fight, one that loses it about 45%. Log lines say "HONEST WORTH: first round won X% ...".

What to watch: fewer long-shot charges (under 55%), the same or more sure charges (90%+), less lost per game, and charges that earn
about what their line promised.
