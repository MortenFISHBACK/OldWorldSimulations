# Flank fixes on v off

Four changes meant to cut the flank and rear charges the computer walks into or leaves itself open to:
1. BEST OVERALL - when a safer choice (turn to face, step back, reform) has the best overall figure by more than the tie band, it is taken; its own risk no longer has to be lower.
2. STOPS SHORT OF THE FLANK - a planned move that ends open to a flank or rear charge is also weighed cut short, 1" at a time.
3. BIG CHARACTER SAFETY - a lone character of 300+ points takes the lower-risk choice unless its job is clearly worth more.
4. PIVOT GUARDS FLANK - no free Lumbering / Quick Turn pivot after a move when the pivot would show a flank or rear to a new charger.

The on-1..4 games play with all four on; off-1..4 are the same games with all four switched off.

Watch for:
- flank and rear charges on units that were free to turn (last batch 1.46 a game): fewer with the fixes on?
- units stopping short or turning to face where they used to walk into a flank charge;
- whether the extra caution costs charges or VP;
- the new "Off best figure" table: every pick below the best figure, with its reason.
