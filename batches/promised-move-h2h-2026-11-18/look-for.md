# Promised moves against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, eighth try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: a unit that
was priced from a spot "move there, charge next turn" is always offered that spot in its move phase and takes it on equal sums; a
promise counts a friend that will charge or shoot the same target first; a promised charge is credited only in the turn the unit's own
sum would make it (no endless waiting); shot danger counts the unit's own points by its own wounds, characters apart. The other side
plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: does a unit that promised a charge make the promised move, and does the charge then come? Are charges on offer still
put off turn after turn? Does the danger a unit expected match what it then lost in the enemy turn?
