# Shooting plan head-to-head (2026-11-15)

The same 144 games twice, the same pairs as today's earlier head-to-heads. In every game ONE side plays with the new army-wide shooting
plan and the other side plays as today's main. In the first half the plan plays Mine, in the second half Opponent.

The change: the army makes one shooting plan each turn. Shooters and missile wizards are shared out over a list of targets so that
together they kill what matters most (counting real wound spread, not averages), fire in order and re-plan after every volley, keep the
same target over turns, and fall back to back-up targets when the main one cannot be hurt. Enemy war machines are main targets.

Watch for: which side wins more often and by how much; war machines and missiles going at the enemy's most dangerous unit when it is free;
whether that unit is dead by round 3; several shooters finishing one unit in the same phase; the "Rules off" line in each game saying
which side played without the plan.
