# Charge outcomes against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the simpler pricing. One side also counts four charge outcomes it used to miss: the target giving ground or falling
back (tag "BREAK OUTCOMES"), an overrun or pursuit into a fresh enemy ("OVERRUN ON"), the target's Stand & Shoot volley
("STAND AND SHOOT"), and the target fleeing from the charge ("FLEES FROM CHARGE").

What to watch: does that side charge more often into fights it wins but cannot wipe out, avoid charges into a gunline's volley
(Dwarfs above all), and use charges to make weak units flee? Look at net points per charged fight, first round won, and points lost.
