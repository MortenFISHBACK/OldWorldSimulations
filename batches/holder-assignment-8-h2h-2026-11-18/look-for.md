# Army-wide objective holders, batch 8: a walk's risk counted at each stop on the way (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it, with every objective counted by the odds. New in this try: when a unit walks to
a marker two or more turns away, its chance of being charged off on the way is counted at each place it stops on the way, not only
where this turn's move ends. Before, hanging back looked safe and going forward paid its risk again for every turn. The other side has
all of this off and picks holders one unit at a time.

- Do units walking to a marker keep the same marker the turn after, move onto it, arrive when they said, and score?
- Is the chance a walk claims now near what happens?
- Do table quarter, special feature and Herdstone claims still come true?
