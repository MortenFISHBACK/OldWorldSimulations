# Charge odds + charge band head-to-head (2026-11-15)

One side plays with two changes; the other side plays as main.
1. Honest charge odds: the six fixes to how the computer figures its chance of winning a charge (joint charges know a challenge is
   coming, refusing a challenge retires the refuser, blows go where aimed, enemy spells in the fight count, a slain charger has lost,
   a wider spread of results).
2. The charge band: a charge that wins 55-75% alone now asks a friend for help when together they win 76% or more (and 15 points more
   than alone), but never when together it becomes overkill. Log lines say "CHARGE BAND: help asked at X% alone to win Y% together".

What to watch: fewer 60-75% charges made alone, more joint charges in the 76-89% band, no new overkill piles, and sure charges (90%+)
winning about as often as they say.
