# Try 11: the army table with joint charges that find room, against simpler pricing (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board and the army-wide move table
(a row per unit, each priced against the others). New in this try: a promised charge reads what the table says is left of the target,
support is worked out from the board, a second charger is priced by the room it has to land, a joint charge is priced only for the
units that find room to land, a moving unit can join a friend's charge when that is worth more ("look back"), and objective markers
are priced at 30 + 60 x the chance the marker is kept through the enemy's turn. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: does a promised charge come? Do two units charge one target together, and do both get in as claimed? Is a second
charger ever halted by the lead? Are held markers kept as claimed? Do the points won per charged fight match what was expected?
