# Standing still by numbers against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play simpler pricing (advance worth, idle cost, risk once, one sum) plus the split log. One side also plays "standing still
by numbers": a unit that stays pays only what its spot falls short of earning ("FALLS SHORT"), no idle cost while its army out-shoots
the enemy ("TIME ON OUR SIDE"), an objective counts only for the cheapest unit that can hold it ("CHEAPEST HOLDER"), a move that ends
less than 1" nearer still serves its job ("NEARER NOT NEEDED"), and a unit with no job is no longer sent at the nearest enemy
("NO FORCED ADVANCE"). The other side chooses as before.

What to watch: does the standing-still side play better (VP margin)? Look for units that stand still and what they earned there,
cheap units holding objectives while dear ones fight, and units that used to walk at the enemy with no reason.
