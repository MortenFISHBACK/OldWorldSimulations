# Army-wide objective holders, fifth try: baggage trains and table quarters by the odds (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it, a marker worth 30 now plus 60 times the chance of keeping it. New in this try:
from round 5 a baggage train is worth its points at the end times the chance the unit is on it and keeps it (nothing before round 5),
and in Domination games from round 5 every unit counts what it adds to its table quarter at the end (controlled, more than double,
uncontested, each by its chance). The other side has both of these off and picks holders one unit at a time.

- Does the side with quarters by the odds score more table-quarter points than the old side?
- When a unit claims a quarter outcome (controlled / more than double / uncontested), does it happen at the end?
- Are baggage trains held at the end more often, and do claimed trains score?
- Do markers still score as often as in the last try?
