# Fight odds 1: the exact first round of a fight, on against off (2026-11-19)

Both sides play the same build and both use the simpler pricing. The only difference: one side works out the first round of a fight
from the actual dice, result by result (who wins, by how much, does the loser break), instead of a bell curve around the average.
In a test of 39 fixed fights, 300 real-dice games each, the new figure matched the dice in 20 fights (5 before).

What to watch: when the new side charges, does the first round go the way it claimed (won, combat result, enemy breaks), band by band?
Does it charge better fights: net points per charged fight, points lost per game, free units caught in flank or rear?
Known not fixed yet: charges by two units together, a champion refusing or retiring from a challenge, fleeing after a lost fight.
