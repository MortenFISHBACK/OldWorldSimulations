# Army-wide objective holders (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army now draws up one table each turn, and at each
deployment drop, that gives every objective marker (and the King of the Hill hill) to the unit that gives up least to hold it; the
other units leave that objective to it, and a marker nobody gains by holding stays empty. The other side picks its holders one unit
at a time, with the old fixed lines (no unit of 210 points or more, no non-Core unit before round 5, hand over at 60%).

What to watch: are more marker turns scored on the new side, and by cheaper units? Does the unit the table names actually reach its
marker and score it? Does a dear unit still walk onto a marker while a cheap Core unit stands idle?
