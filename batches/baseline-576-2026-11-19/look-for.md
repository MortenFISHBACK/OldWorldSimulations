# Baseline on the new main (2026-11-19), every new part on

This batch is the new point of comparison. Main now holds the overhaul (correct odds, five options, joint charges, army-wide planner,
missiles on one target, support adds), the army-wide objective holders (no late cut on far trips) and the fix that stops formed units
sliding sideways during a normal move or march. On the sites all of these are off; in this batch every one is ON for both sides
(Medium against Medium), 288 tournament pairs, seeds 1 and 2, scenarios mixed: 576 games.

What to watch: claimed against happened for charges (first round won when charging), promised charges, far objective walks and
held markers; points lost per game, units caught in flank or rear, and any game that stalls or crashes. Later builds are measured
against these games.
