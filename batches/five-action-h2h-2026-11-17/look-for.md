# Five actions against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the simpler pricing (advance worth, idle cost, risk once, one sum). One side also plays the new "five actions" way of
choosing (tag "FIVE ACTION"): every unit weighs doing nothing, shooting, moving, marching and charging by one sum - this turn, next
turn's best thing times its chance, help to friends, minus risk and the cost of standing idle. Its charges play the fight out over three
rounds in points ("FOUR ROUNDS"), count run-downs by the pursuit dice ("RUN DOWN") and count the enemy's charges by the points they take
("CHARGE LOSSES"). The other side chooses as before.

What to watch: is the five-action side clearly better? Look for charges that win, units that stand idle or march off, units that avoid
charges they would lose, and objectives and the Herdstone held by cheap units.
