# Deployment by the figures against main (2026-11-17)

Both sides play main's computer opponent. One side also deploys by the figures: cheap units set up to hold objective markers
("HOLDER AT DEPLOYMENT"), big lone characters pick their lane by the numbers ("LANE BY FIGURES"), and every spot, screen,
hold-up and the drop order are weighed by the approach figures ("DEPLOY BY FIGURES"). The "no spot given" fix is on for both.

What to watch: does that side hold its own markers from the start, meet the enemy's big units on better terms, and win more of the
fights it starts? Does a holder's figure (up to about 230 VP) pull too many units onto markers? Do joined characters (Orion) still deploy?
