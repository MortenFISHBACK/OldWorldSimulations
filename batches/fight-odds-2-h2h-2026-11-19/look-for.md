# Fight odds 2: the new fight estimate against the old one (2026-11-19)

Both sides play the same build and both use the simpler pricing. The only difference is how a side works out a fight before it charges.
The new side works out the first round from the dice result by result, gives one joint figure when several units fight one enemy, and
reads a challenge the way the game fights it (a duellist's later blows are lost once his opponent falls, a slain duellist in a unit is
one model and not the whole unit, and the charger expects the challenge the enemy would really pick). The old side uses the estimate
from before these fixes.

What to watch: when the new side charges, does the first round go the way it claimed, band by band? Fights with a challenge, and a lone
character or monster charging a unit: do the claims hold, and does a lone hero still take charges it claims it will almost surely lose?
Joint charges: claimed v won. Then net points per charged fight, points lost per game, free units caught in flank or rear.
