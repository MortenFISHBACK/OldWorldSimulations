# Job framework head-to-head 2, with the five charge fixes (2026-11-14)

The same 144 games twice, and in every game only ONE side plays with the new job framework (best spot for each unit: shooting, next turn's
charge, holding, support, minus risk; screens, redirects and lures only when they work this turn; the defensive army; the lost charge;
random movers by their figures; stable roles; best facing). The other side plays as before the framework. In the first half the
framework plays Mine, in the second half Opponent.

Watch for: which side wins more often and by how much; the framework side moving to a spot from which it charges next turn while the
other side walks straight at the enemy; the framework side's chaff screening only when it really blocks shooting; the "Rules off" line
in each game saying which side played without the framework.

New in this batch: the framework side now values waiting after its costs, charges at once when it reaches 75%+ and the charge is worth
something, offers Support less often, lets war engines charge at 60% to win, and calls an army defensive only with enough shooting.
Watch whether the framework side now charges about as often as the other side, and whether its deployment looks normal (the deployment
fault of batch 1 is fixed).
