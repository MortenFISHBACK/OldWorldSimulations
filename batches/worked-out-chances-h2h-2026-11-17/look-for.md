# Worked-out chances against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the simpler pricing. One side also uses two worked-out chances: its charge jobs come only from the game's own list of
legal charges (tag "LEGAL CHARGES ONLY"; a charge left off says why, and a planned charge the list does not offer says "CHARGE NOT
DECLARED"), and a shot and a friend's charge on the same target are priced together ("SHOT OR CHARGE").

What to watch: does that side stop planning charges it cannot declare, and stop shooting at a target a friend was going to charge?
Look at net points per charged fight, first round won, points lost, and charges left off because a friend blocks the way (above all
the second unit of a joint charge).
