# Set-ups into charges head-to-head (2026-11-16)

One side plays with three changes; the other side plays as main.
1. Set-up spot: a "close in to charge next turn" job is priced at, and moved to, a spot the unit can really reach this turn,
   facing so that next turn's charge is really on. Log lines say "SET-UP SPOT".
2. Carries out close-in: a unit that closed in alone last turn charges that target first this turn if still worth it and 50%+ to win
   the first round. Log lines say "CARRIES OUT CLOSE-IN".
3. Joint as one: friends count in a set-up group only if their own move can really arrive. Log lines say "JOINT AS ONE".
Both sides also log "NO CHARGE WHY" (why a unit that closed in has no charge on that target), which changes no decision.

What to watch: more close-in moves that end in charge range and facing, more of them that charge their target next turn (was 15%),
more joint charges, the same or more charged fights per game, and no slower Movement phases.
