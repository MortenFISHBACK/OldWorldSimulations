# Shooting plan with the gun fixes, head-to-head (2026-11-16 afternoon)

The same shooting-heavy pairs as this morning (144 games, played twice). In every game ONE side plays the full shooting plan, its
target fixes AND the four new gun fixes; the other side plays as today's main (none of them). In the first half the plan plays Mine,
in the second half Opponent.

## The four gun fixes

- Gun figures: Organ Guns, Helblasters, bolt throwers and other guns that roll to hit are now judged by what they really do.
- Gun mask: a move that blocks the sight of the army's own guns costs what the gun can no longer shoot.
- Gun view: a war machine is deployed where it sees the best target.
- Gun threat: a gun prefers the enemy unit that could destroy it next turn.

Watch for:
- Which side wins more often and by how much (this morning, without the gun fixes: +74 VP for the plan, within noise).
- War machines shooting the enemy's most dangerous units, and own units standing out of their line of sight.
- Guns lost to charges.
- The "Rules off" line in each game saying which side played without the plan and the gun fixes.
