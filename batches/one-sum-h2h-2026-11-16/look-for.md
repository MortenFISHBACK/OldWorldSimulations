# Simpler pricing head-to-head (2026-11-16)

One side plays with seven changes on; the other side plays as main (all seven off). The changes: the advance figures
("ADVANCE WORTH"), a cost for standing still ("IDLE COST"), no shooting risk on a unit that is likely charged anyway ("RISK ONCE"),
and four "one sum" rules that count each part of a move's worth once and the same way in the plan and in the move
("ONE FIGHT FORMULA", "COVER ONCE", "CHAFF IN THE MOVE", "ONE OBJECTIVE FORMULA").

What to watch: the change is meant to be simpler at about the same strength. Look for units that stand around doing nothing,
advances that never fight, chaff that screens and redirects, and objectives held; the VP margin should be about the same or better.
