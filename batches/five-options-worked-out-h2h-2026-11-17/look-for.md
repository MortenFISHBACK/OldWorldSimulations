# Five options with worked-out chances against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five-option pricing with fixed shares replaced by chances worked out from the board
(switch "worked-out-chances": legal charges only, shot or charge, move to charge worked out from distance and movement, an enemy charge
is a risk only when that enemy expects to win, the enemy's shooting in points, and the cost of standing still worked out from the
board). The other side plays simpler pricing.

What to watch: are the odds right? For each job (charge now, move to charge, shoot, stand, support) what the computer expected against
what happened; whether enemy charges it feared actually came; how often units stand still and what they were doing; charges planned but
never declared; shots planned that never fired.
