# Challenge in the fight head-to-head (2026-11-22)

What changed: when the computer plays out a fight it is thinking of taking, it now reads every round with the challenge the
enemy unit would issue or accept (its champion or a character stepping up), as the game fights it. Before, the played-out fight
started from the round without the challenge, so a lone hero or lord charging a unit with a champion was counted as cutting
down the ranks when in the game he fights the champion and the rest of his blows are lost. Found in game L0122: a Tomb King on his
own charged a block of Gors, the figure said he wins 83%, he lost both rounds and died.

The same 72 pairs are played both ways: once with the rule off for one army, once off for the other. Every other rule is on for
both sides (today's main).

What to watch: the margin of the side with the rule against the side without it; lone characters charging units with a champion
or characters (fewer such charges, fewer characters lost); points claimed for charges against what the targets really gave.
