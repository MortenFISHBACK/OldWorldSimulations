# Promised marches against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, ninth try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: the march
check weighs a march by the same sum as every other option (march by the sum), so a unit that promised "march there, charge next turn"
should now make that march; a fight is priced for every round the battle has left, not only three (fight to the end); when a promised
spot is in a friend's way or line of fire, the unit with the dearer sum keeps its plan; and a charge priced two or more turns out
now counts that the target may reform to meet it. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: does a unit that promised a charge make the promised march or move, and does the charge then come? Are charges on
offer still put off turn after turn? Do the points won per charged fight match what was expected?
