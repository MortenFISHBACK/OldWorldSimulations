# Joint charge pricing against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the simpler pricing. One side also prices a second charger by what it adds to a friend's charge (tag "SECOND
CHARGER ADDS"): a unit joins a charge when the fight together is worth more than it is alone, and a friend is offered as help
whenever it adds to the fight, not only when the first charger would lose alone. Both sides log how much the best friend in reach
would have added ("FRIEND IN REACH") and whether last turn's pair set-ups led to a joint charge ("PAIR SET-UP").

What to watch: does the joint-charge side make more charges with two units on one enemy, win the first round more often, and lose
fewer points? Look for second chargers that arrive, enemies broken by two units, and units drawn away from objectives to help.
