## Battle groups: groups that attack, on v off (12 November)
- 144 DKTC games twice: with the battle groups on (this branch) and the same games with them off (`--rules-off battle-groups`), Hero v Hero.
- New: in turns 1-3 a block and its cheap fast escort pick one enemy they beat together and close in on it so both can charge it in the same
  turn (GROUP AIM). Watch for escorts that stay beside their block on the way in, and blocks and escorts charging the same enemy together.
- New measures: do promised counter-charges come (a friend charging whoever charged a covered unit), and if not, why.
- Watch for games that stop early, units that stand all game, or escorts that run off alone.
