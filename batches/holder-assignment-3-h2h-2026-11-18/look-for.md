# Army-wide objective holders, third try: HOLD ODDS (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it. New in this try: holding a marker (or the hill) is worth the points now plus
60 times the chance the unit keeps it through the enemy's turn (not charged off, not out-stood), worked out from the board. A unit one
turn away counts 30 times its chance of arriving; two or more turns away counts nothing. The other side picks holders one unit at a time.

- When the table says a unit holds a marker, does it still hold it after the enemy's turn as often as it claimed?
- Are more marker turns scored on the new side than on the old side?
- Do walk-ons one turn away actually arrive and score the next round?
- Does a marker far from every unit stay empty all game more often on the new side?
