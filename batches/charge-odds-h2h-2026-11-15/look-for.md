# Charge odds head-to-head (2026-11-15)

One side plays with six fixes to how the computer figures its chance of winning a charge; the other side plays as main.
The fixes: joint charges know a challenge is coming; refusing a challenge retires the refuser; blows go where they are aimed
(lone characters and monsters); enemy spells cast in the fight count; a charger wiped out has lost; the spread of results is wider.

What to watch: sure charges (90%+) should now win about as often as they say, also when characters join, challenges are issued
or spells are cast in the fight. The side with the fixes should make fewer over-confident charges that lose.
