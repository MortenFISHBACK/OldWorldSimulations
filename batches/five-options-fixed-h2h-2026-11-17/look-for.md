# Fixed five options against simpler pricing (2026-11-17, second try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board, after the fix for the
stopped batch: a move "to charge next turn" is only promised on a target that is in charge range from the new spot, that charge is
priced the same way as a charge made now, and the target is expected to step away by its own best move. The other side plays the
simpler pricing.

What to watch: are the odds right? When a unit moves to charge next turn, does the charge come? When it charges now, does the fight go
the way it expected (target destroyed, charger lost)? How often each side charges, and how often units stand still and why.
