# Game-list odds against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, fifth try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: an enemy
counts as a charge danger to a unit only when his own charge list would offer that charge, and as a shooting danger only when his own
target list would hold the unit where it stands. The fixed "turn worth" cost on a charge is gone (the turns a charge ties a unit up are
counted in the sum). On both sides: the charge figure uses the charge list's own sight check, and skirmishers need more than half
their models to see. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: does the danger a unit expected match what it then lost in the enemy turn? Were units priced as safe then charged or
shot anyway? When a unit moves to charge next turn, does the charge come?
