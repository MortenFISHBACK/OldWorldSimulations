# Cut C head-to-head (2026-11-21)

Cut C takes three repeats out of the computer's thinking: the second plan no longer plays the enemy's turn again after the charges
(the run made before the moves stands), the older guess of one move per enemy unit is not made, and the end-of-turn replay of the
enemy's turn is kept out of a player's wait (simulations still make it, for the record, on both sides). One side has the cut, the other
the full thinking; the same pairs are played both ways. On 8 turns of Morten's games it saved about two thirds of the thinking and
played the same in 5 of 8.

Watch: whether the side with the cut wins less (VP margin), its thinking time per turn, and turns where its moves differ from what the
full thinking would have done (second-plan rows, the one-move guess lines "HIS ONE MOVE" missing on the cut side).
