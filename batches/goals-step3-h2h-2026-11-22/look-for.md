# Goals-first step 3 head-to-head (2026-11-22)

Step 3 of goals-first planning: a unit walking to a goal takes the spot from which its charge comes soonest (GOAL ON TIME), on equal
sums the spot nearer its goal (NEAREST TO GOAL), it is offered a move straight at its goal's target (GOAL ROUTE), and units on foot
pick their move before units that fly (FOOT FIRST). One side has the four on, the other has them off; the same 144 pairs, both ways.
The speed cuts are on for both sides.

Watch: VP margin of the side with step 3; units with a goal that march their full move instead of short steps; goals that end in the
planned charge; fliers that wait for foot units; minutes a game.
