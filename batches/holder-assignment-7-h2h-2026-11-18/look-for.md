# Army-wide objective holders, batch 7: walks counted on the real ground, one quarter per enemy unit (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it, with every objective counted by the odds. New in this try: a unit's walk to a
far marker is counted in the turns its real moves need from where it stands and the way it faces (not a straight line at full march),
and in table quarters each enemy unit is counted in one quarter only. Each move row now says which objective a move goes onto and how
it ranks. The other side has all of this off and picks holders one unit at a time.
