# Same charge later against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, seventh try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: an enemy whose
shooting is worth more to him than a charge is no charge danger; an enemy that moves at random gets in by its own dice; a charge now also
counts the charge the enemy unit would have made on another of our units; and a charge on offer now that is put off for the same charge
next turn no longer counts this turn's shots and spells as a gain of waiting. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: are charges on offer now still put off for the same charge next turn, and does the charge then come? Does the side
now pick charges it is likely to lose? Does the danger a unit expected match what it then lost in the enemy turn?
