# One-figure charges against simpler pricing (2026-11-17, third try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board, now with ONE figure for
charges: a charge is kept or dropped on the same played-out fight it is ranked by, a charge promised for next turn goes through the
same steps as a charge on offer now, and no fight round is counted that the battle no longer has. The other side plays the simpler
pricing.

What to watch: are the odds right? When a unit moves to charge next turn, does the charge come? When it charges now, does it win the
first round, destroy the target, lose the charger? Charges that rarely win round one may now be made when their three rounds come out
ahead: watch first round won when charging. Targets that flee from the charge are not yet counted in the odds.
