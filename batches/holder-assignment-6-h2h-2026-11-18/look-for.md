# Army-wide objective holders, batch 6: walks that are carried out, quarter rulings, buffs by the odds (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it, with every objective counted by the odds. New in this try: when two moves are
worth the same, the unit takes the spot nearest its objective; a unit that starts a walk to a marker keeps the round it promised to be
there (one round late counts only the last moment, two rounds late nothing, and the marker may go to another unit); in table
quarters enemy units that could still walk in count by their chance and a quarter's worth is shared by our units in it; the special
feature's and Herdstone's buffs each turn count by the dice and the board. The other side has all of this off and picks holders one
unit at a time. Each claim is written on the game log row (HOLD ODDS, QUARTER ODDS, WALK PROMISE).

- Do units walking to a marker two or more turns away arrive when they said, and score?
- How often does a walk run late or pass to another unit?
- When a unit claims it walks onto or keeps the special feature or the Herdstone, does it score?
- Do table quarter and baggage train claims come true?
