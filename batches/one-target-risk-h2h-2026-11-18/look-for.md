# One-target risk against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, fourth try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: an enemy
shooter now counts as a danger only to the one unit it would most like to shoot, and an enemy charger only to the one charge it would
most like to make (before, one enemy was counted in full against every unit it could reach). A target may also reform to meet a charge
promised for next turn. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: are the odds right? Does the danger a unit expected match what it then lost in the enemy turn? Units told an enemy
"has a better target": were they then shot or charged by it anyway? When a unit moves to charge next turn, does the charge come?
