# Try 12: the army table carried out and made again, against simpler pricing (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board and the army-wide move table
(a row per unit, each priced against the others). New in this try: the move phase carries out what the table decided at the start of the
turn; after the charges the table is made again on the real board; shooting is chosen after the moves; a promised charge counts only the
reach left after the enemy's one best move; and a champion may retire when a challenge is refused (both sides). The other side plays the
simpler pricing.

What to watch: does a promised charge now come? Does the table made again keep or withdraw its promises? Do two units charge one target
together and both get in? Are held markers kept as claimed? Do the points won per charged fight match what was expected?
