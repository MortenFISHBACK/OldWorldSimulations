# Try 15: a promised charge carried out step by step, against simpler pricing (2026-11-19)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board and the army-wide move table.
New in this try: the unit marches where the move table told it to (no separate march check overriding it); our own shots and spells
at a target we promised to charge count the lost promise as a cost, and a promise counts the spells it casts itself; the chance of a
promised charge is the product of named steps read from the board (shown as "PROMISE IN STEPS" on each promise); a Reserve move counts
the promise it gives up. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: do promised charges now come (try 14: 88 claimed in 100, about 26 came)? Does the claim fall toward what happened in
each band? How often does a promising unit end off its spot (try 14: 14 in 100)? How long does the new side think?
