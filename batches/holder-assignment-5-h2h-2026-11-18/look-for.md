# Army-wide objective holders, sixth try: special feature, Herdstone and far markers by the odds (2026-11-18)

Both sides play the same new pricing with worked-out chances. On one side the army draws up one table each turn that gives every
objective to the unit that gives up least to hold it. New in this try: the special feature and the Herdstone are worth their points
at the end times the chance the unit is on it and keeps it to the end (an enemy unit that can end nearer takes it), counted from
round 5 for a unit standing on it and from round 4 for one walking there. A marker further away counts what it scores from the turn
the unit arrives times the chance it gets there. Baggage trains and table quarters are by the odds from round 5, as before. The other
side has all of this off and picks holders one unit at a time. Each claim is written on the game log row (HOLD ODDS, QUARTER ODDS).

- Is the special feature held at the end more often than by the old side (last try: 21 against 35 of 63 games)?
- When a unit claims it keeps the feature or the Herdstone, does it score?
- Do units walking to a marker two or more turns away arrive and score, and are far markers left empty less often?
- Do baggage train and table quarter claims come true?
