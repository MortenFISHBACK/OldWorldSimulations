# Staying to shoot head-to-head (2026-11-15)

The same 144 games twice. In every game ONE side plays with the new shooting and missile changes and the other side plays as today's
main. In the first half the changes play Mine, in the second half Opponent.

The changes: shooters and missile wizards value the volleys of the next two turns when they choose to stay or move; they take their best
move without marching when they can still shoot or cast; a shot at a big enemy counts what that enemy would do to the army; shooters and
wizards share one kill over this turn and the next; a wizard turns to face its best missile target when that opens no flank; missile
wizards stand first in a unit with several characters.

Watch for: which side wins more often and by how much; the changed side's gunlines standing to shoot instead of marching; war machines
and missiles going at the enemy's most dangerous unit; the "Rules off" line in each game saying which side played without the changes.
