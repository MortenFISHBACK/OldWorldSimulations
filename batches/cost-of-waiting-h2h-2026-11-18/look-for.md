# Cost of waiting against simpler pricing (2026-11-18, sixth try)

Both sides play the same build. One side uses the five options with chances worked out from the board. New in this try: when a
unit plans to charge next turn, the target is expected to make its own best legal move first (it may move away, turn or reform); a
shooter that can move and then shoot counts by that shot; a charge now also counts what the charged unit's shooters and wizards can no
longer do to our other units in the enemy turn; and a charge planned for next turn on a target a friend charges now is cut the same
way the charge now is. The other side plays the simpler pricing.

What to watch: when a unit moves to charge next turn, does the charge come? Are charges on offer now still put off for the same
charge next turn? Does the danger a unit expected match what it then lost in the enemy turn?
