# Honest next-turn chances against simpler pricing (2026-11-17)

Both sides play the simpler pricing. One side also prices honestly: next turn's charge counts only at the chance it really happens
(tag "MEASURED NEXT CHARGE", about a fifth of the old figure), and a broken enemy counts by the chance it really ends as points
(tag "RUN DOWN", about three in four mid-table). Both sides write the split log (JOB SPLIT, MOVE SPLIT, outcome chances on every charge).

What to watch: does the honest side stop walking units forward for charges that never come, and charge broken or weak enemies
it can really finish? Look for fewer marches toward far targets, more units staying on objectives or shooting, and whether it loses
fewer points while still winning its charges.
