# Join bonus head-to-head (2026-11-16)

One side plays with the new "bonus for joining a friend's charge"; the other side plays as main (bonus switched off).
When a friend already charges (or is set to charge) an enemy unit worth 250+ points, joining that charge counts extra:
+21 VP per 100 points of the joiner x the chance both arrive. Log lines say "JOINS A FRIEND".

What to watch: more joint charges on 250+ point targets, those fights won more often and earning more per 100 charger points,
no more overkill on cheap targets, and the same or better VP margin.
