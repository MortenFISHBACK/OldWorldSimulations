# Shooting target fixes head-to-head (2026-11-16)

The same 144 games twice, only pairs where at least one army shoots a lot (3 or more war machines, or 30% of its points in shooting).
In every game ONE side plays the full shooting plan with this morning's three fixes, the other side plays as today's main
(no shooting plan at all). In the first half the plan plays Mine, in the second half Opponent.

## Shooting plan with the target fixes

The army makes one shooting plan each turn, and now: every wound counts towards a kill (not just kills and halvings), the danger of
each enemy unit is judged by what it can really reach and kill, and a shot is valued the same way as a charge at the same unit.

- Which side wins more often and by how much.
- War machines shooting at the enemy's big, dangerous units instead of cheap ones.
- Units with bows and guns still joining charges together with others.
- The "Rules off" line in each game saying which side played without the plan.
